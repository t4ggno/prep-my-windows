Controls whether **Brave** keeps running in the background after you close it.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Brave keeps running after its last window is closed. |
| **Off** | `0` | Brave exits completely. |

> **Good to know:** Browser policies make the browser show *"managed by your organization"* in its settings.
