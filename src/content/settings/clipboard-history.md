Keeps a **clipboard history** that you open with **Win + V**.

### Why it matters
- Paste anything you copied earlier, pin frequent snippets and paste emoji or symbols.
- The history stays on this PC; cloud sync is a separate setting.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Win + V shows your recent clipboard items. |
| **Off** | `0` | Windows default. Only the last copied item is kept. |

> **Good to know:** The history is cleared when you restart, except for pinned items.
