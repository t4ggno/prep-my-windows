Controls **optional connected experiences** in Microsoft 365 apps, such as 3D Maps in Excel and online pictures.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** | `2` | Optional connected experiences are unavailable. |
| **On** | `1` | Office default. They are available. |

> **Good to know:** Coauthoring, OneDrive storage and other core connected experiences keep working. Applies to the current user.
