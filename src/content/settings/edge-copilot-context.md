Stops **Copilot** from reading the content of the page you are viewing, for consumer accounts.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Copilot may use page content to answer questions. |
| **Off** | `0` | Copilot cannot read page content. |

> **Good to know:** Requires Edge 124 or newer.
