Stops Edge from downloading and running a **local AI model** for on-device AI features.

### Why it matters
- The model takes several gigabytes of disk space and is downloaded in the background.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `0` | Edge default. The model may be downloaded and used. |
| **Off** | `1` | No local model is downloaded. |
