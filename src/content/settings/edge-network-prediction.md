**Network prediction** lets Edge preload pages and resolve addresses it expects you to visit.

### Why it matters
- Preloading contacts sites you never actually open.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Always** | `0` | Edge default. Edge predicts and preloads on any network. |
| **Never** | `2` | No network prediction. |

> **Good to know:** Pages may open a little slower with **Never**.
