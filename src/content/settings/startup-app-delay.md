Removes the **delay** Windows adds before starting your startup apps after sign-in.

### Why it matters
- Windows waits about ten seconds before launching startup apps; tools like tray utilities appear sooner without it.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No delay** | `0` | Startup apps launch right after sign-in. |
| **Windows default** | Not set | Startup apps are delayed. |
