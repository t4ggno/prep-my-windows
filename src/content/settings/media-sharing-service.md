The **Windows Media Player Network Sharing** service (`WMPNetworkSvc`) shares your Media Player library with other devices on the network.

### Why it matters
- Rarely used, and it advertises your media library on the local network.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Automatic** | `2` | Starts with Windows. |
| **Manual** | `3` | Windows default. Starts when sharing is used. |
| **Disabled** | `4` | Never starts. |

> **Good to know:** Keep it if you stream music or videos from this PC to a TV or console with the classic Media Player.
