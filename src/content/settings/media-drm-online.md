Stops **Windows Media DRM** from contacting the internet to acquire licences or upgrade its components.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `0` | Windows default. DRM may go online. |
| **Off** | `1` | DRM stays offline. |

> **Good to know:** Old protected WMA or WMV files may no longer play.
