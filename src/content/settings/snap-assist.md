**Snap Assist** suggests other windows to fill the free space after you snap a window to one side.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Windows default. Suggestions appear after snapping. |
| **Off** | `0` | Windows snap without suggestions. |

> **Good to know:** This rule is optional. Snapping itself keeps working.
