Controls whether **Mozilla Firefox** sends telemetry data to Mozilla.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `0` | Firefox default. Telemetry is sent. |
| **Off** | `1` | No telemetry is sent. |

> **Good to know:** Browser policies make the browser show *"managed by your organization"* in its settings.
