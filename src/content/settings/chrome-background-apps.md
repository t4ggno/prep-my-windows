Controls whether **Google Chrome** keeps running in the background after you close it.

### Why it matters
- Background Chrome processes keep using memory and CPU after the browser is closed.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Chrome keeps running after its last window is closed. |
| **Off** | `0` | Chrome exits completely. |

> **Good to know:** Browser policies make the browser show *"managed by your organization"* in its settings.
