**Microsoft consumer experiences** install suggested apps and games (such as Candy Crush or TikTok) and show related promotions.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `0` | Windows default. Suggested apps may be installed automatically. |
| **Off** | `1` | No automatic app suggestions or installs. |

> **Good to know:** Windows only fully honours this policy on Enterprise and Education. The other suggestion rules in this category cover Home and Pro.
