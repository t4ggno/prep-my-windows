The time your **active hours** end. After this time, Windows may restart to finish updates.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **00:00 – 23:00** | `0` – `23` | The hour active hours end. |

> **Good to know:** Active hours must be between 1 and 18 hours long, so end times outside that range cannot be picked.
