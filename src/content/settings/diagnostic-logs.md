Limits **diagnostic log collection** that Windows performs on top of optional diagnostic data.

### Why it matters
- Prevents additional log files from being gathered and uploaded for troubleshooting at Microsoft.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** | `1` | Additional diagnostic logs are not collected. |
| **On** | `0` | Windows default. Logs may be collected with optional data. |
