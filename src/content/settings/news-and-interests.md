Allows or blocks **news and interests**, the Windows 10 taskbar weather and news feed.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | The news feed is allowed. |
| **Off** | `0` | The news feed is blocked. |

> **Good to know:** Windows protects this value with **User Choice Protection**. The rule only works after that driver is off and Windows was restarted.
