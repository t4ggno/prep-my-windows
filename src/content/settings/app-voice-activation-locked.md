Controls whether apps can **listen for a voice keyword while the PC is locked**.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User decides** | `0` | Windows default. You control access in Settings. |
| **Force allow** | `1` | All apps may listen while locked. |
| **Deny** | `2` | No app listens while the PC is locked. |
