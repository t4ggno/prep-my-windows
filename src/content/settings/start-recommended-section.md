Hides the entire **Recommended** section in Start, giving the space to pinned apps.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `0` | Windows default. Start shows the Recommended section. |
| **Off** | `1` | The Recommended section is removed. |

> **Good to know:** Requires Windows 11 22H2 (build 22621) on Pro, Enterprise or Education.
