Decides whether the PC stays connected to the network during **Modern Standby** (sleep) while it runs on battery.

### Why it matters
- Network activity during sleep is a common cause of laptops that are empty or warm in the bag.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | The network stays connected during standby. |
| **Off** | `0` | The network disconnects during standby. |

> **Good to know:** Only affects PCs with Modern Standby (S0).
