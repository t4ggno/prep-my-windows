Stops **search and site suggestions** while you type in the Edge address bar.

### Why it matters
- Every keystroke in the address bar is otherwise sent to your search engine.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Edge default. Suggestions come from your search engine. |
| **Off** | `0` | Only history and favourites are suggested. |
