Makes **Recall** available or unavailable on Copilot+ PCs. Recall takes regular screenshots of your screen and makes them searchable with AI.

### Why it matters
- Snapshots can capture passwords, private messages and documents.
- With **Off**, Windows removes the Recall component and nobody can turn it on.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Recall can be enabled. |
| **Off** | `0` | Recall is not available on this PC. |
