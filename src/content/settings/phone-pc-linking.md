**Phone-PC linking** lets you continue tasks from your phone on this PC, for example via Phone Link.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Your phone can hand over activities to this PC. |
| **Off** | `0` | Phone-PC linking is turned off. |

> **Good to know:** Set to **On** if you use Phone Link to send links or continue browsing from your phone.
