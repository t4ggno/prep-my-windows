Controls whether Store apps may **run in the background** to receive notifications and stay up to date.

### Why it matters
- Background apps use CPU, memory, network and battery while you are not using them.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User decides** | `0` | Windows default. You control background activity per app. |
| **Force allow** | `1` | All apps may run in the background. |
| **Deny** | `2` | No Store app runs in the background. |

> **Good to know:** Store apps such as Mail or messengers only notify you while they are open when set to **Deny**.
