**Automatic active hours** lets Windows guess when you use your PC and move the active hours on its own.

### Why it matters
- Turning it off keeps the fixed hours below, so update restarts happen when you expect them.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Windows adjusts active hours based on your activity. |
| **Off** | `0` | The fixed start and end times are used. |
