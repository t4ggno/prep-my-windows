Controls how much **diagnostic data** Microsoft 365 apps send to Microsoft.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** | `3` | No diagnostic data about the Office apps is sent. |
| **Required** | `1` | Only the minimum data to keep Office secure and up to date. |
| **Optional** | `2` | Office default. Required and optional data. |

> **Good to know:** Required service data is still sent. Applies to the current user.
