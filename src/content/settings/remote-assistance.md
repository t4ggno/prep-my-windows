**Remote Assistance** lets someone you invite view or control your PC through an invitation file.

### Why it matters
- Turning it off removes an incoming remote access path you probably never use.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Windows default. Remote Assistance invitations are allowed. |
| **Off** | `0` | Remote Assistance connections are refused. |

> **Good to know:** **Quick Assist** and third-party tools like TeamViewer keep working.
