Controls **Brave VPN** and its offers in the toolbar.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `0` | Brave VPN offers are shown. |
| **Off** | `1` | Brave VPN is turned off and hidden. |

> **Good to know:** Browser policies make the browser show *"managed by your organization"* in its settings.
