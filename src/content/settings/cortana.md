Allows or blocks **Cortana**, Microsoft's retired voice assistant, through policy.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Cortana may run. |
| **Off** | `0` | Cortana is blocked. |

> **Good to know:** Mostly relevant for Windows 10 and older Windows 11 builds where Cortana is still installed.
