Controls **Brave Wallet**, Brave's built-in crypto wallet.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `0` | Brave Wallet is available. |
| **Off** | `1` | Brave Wallet is turned off and hidden. |

> **Good to know:** Browser policies make the browser show *"managed by your organization"* in its settings.
