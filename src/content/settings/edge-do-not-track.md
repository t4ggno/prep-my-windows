Sends a **Do Not Track** request with every page you visit.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Websites are asked not to track you. |
| **Off** | `0` | Edge default. No Do Not Track request is sent. |

> **Good to know:** Websites are free to ignore the request; tracking prevention is the stronger protection.
