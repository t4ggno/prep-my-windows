**Delivery Optimization** shares Windows and Store updates with other PCs, like a peer-to-peer network.

### Why it matters
- With internet peers, your upload bandwidth is used to send updates to strangers.
- **Off** downloads updates only from Microsoft.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** | `0` | Downloads only from Microsoft; no sharing. |
| **Local network** | `1` | Windows default. Shares with PCs on your local network. |
| **Local and internet** | `3` | Also shares with PCs on the internet. |

> **Good to know:** **Local network** can save bandwidth in homes with several Windows PCs.
