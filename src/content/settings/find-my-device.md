**Find my device** regularly saves your PC's location to your Microsoft account so you can locate it if it is lost.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Windows default. The location is reported to your account. |
| **Off** | `0` | Find my device is turned off. |

> **Good to know:** Set to **On** for laptops you might lose; it needs location services.
