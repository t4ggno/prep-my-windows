The time your **active hours** begin. Windows does not restart for updates during active hours.

### Why it matters
- Protects your sessions from surprise update restarts.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **00:00 – 23:00** | `0` – `23` | The hour active hours start. |

> **Good to know:** Active hours can span at most 18 hours. Changing the start keeps the length and moves the end with it.
