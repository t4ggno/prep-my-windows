The **Fax** service sends and receives faxes through a fax modem.

### Why it matters
- Almost no PC has a fax modem today; the service only takes up resources.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Automatic** | `2` | Starts with Windows. |
| **Manual** | `3` | Windows default. Starts when a fax app needs it. |
| **Disabled** | `4` | Never starts. |
