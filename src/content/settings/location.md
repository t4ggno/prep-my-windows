The **location policy** for the whole PC. When blocked, no app and no Windows feature can determine where you are.

### Why it matters
- Blocking location also breaks automatic time zones, local weather, maps and *Find my device*.
- The recommendation is to keep it allowed and decide per app in *Settings → Privacy & security → Location*.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Allowed** | Not set | Windows default. Location follows your choices in Settings. |
| **Blocked** | `1` | Location is turned off for everyone and cannot be turned on. |
