**Cross-device experiences** (Connected Devices Platform) let apps continue tasks on your other devices and discover nearby devices.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Apps can share and continue experiences across devices. |
| **Off** | `0` | Cross-device features are turned off. |

> **Good to know:** Nearby sharing and some Phone Link features depend on this platform.
