**Online speech recognition** sends your voice to Microsoft's cloud for dictation and voice typing.

### Values
| Option | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On** | `1` | Voice input is processed in the cloud. |
| **Off** | `0` | Only on-device speech recognition is used. |

> **Good to know:** Voice typing (Win + H) may be less accurate or unavailable in some languages when off.
